# CS-255-Portfolio

## DriverPass Project Reflection

### Briefly summarize the DriverPass project. Who was the client? What type of system did they want you to design?

The client for this project was DriverPass, a company that wanted to provide better training for students preparing for their driving tests. DriverPass wanted a system that would allow customers to create accounts, purchase training packages, take online practice tests, schedule driving lessons, and track their progress. The system also needed to support different users, including customers, driving instructors, secretaries, and administrators. My goal was to design a system that met these needs while also being secure, organized, and easy to use.

### What did you do particularly well?

I think I did particularly well at identifying the different requirements of the DriverPass system and organizing them into a clear design. I was able to separate functional requirements, such as scheduling appointments and taking practice tests, from nonfunctional requirements, such as security, performance, and reliability. I also think my UML diagrams did a good job of showing how the different users would interact with the system and how different parts of the system would work together.

### If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?

If I could revise one part of my work, I would improve some of my UML diagrams. Although the diagrams show the main processes and relationships in the DriverPass system, I would make them more consistent and simplify some of the information so they are easier to understand at a glance. I would also review the relationships between classes and system components to make sure each connection is represented as clearly as possible.

### How did you interpret the user's needs and implement them into your system design? Why is it so important to consider the user's needs when designing?

I interpreted the user's needs by reviewing the information provided by the client and identifying the features that each type of user needed. For example, customers needed to schedule driving lessons and take practice tests, while instructors needed access to their schedules and student information. Administrators required additional permissions to manage users and system information. I used these requirements when creating the UML diagrams and technical requirements. Considering the user's needs is important because a system can function correctly from a technical perspective but still be unsuccessful if it does not solve the problems of the people who actually use it.

### How do you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a system?

My approach to designing software is to first understand the problem and determine what the client and users need from the system. From there, I organize the requirements and identify the major users, processes, and components before creating the design. UML diagrams are useful because they allow me to visualize how users interact with the system and how different components relate to each other. In future projects, I would continue using requirement analysis, use cases, activity diagrams, sequence diagrams, and class diagrams. I would also review the design throughout the development process and make changes when new requirements or problems are identified.
